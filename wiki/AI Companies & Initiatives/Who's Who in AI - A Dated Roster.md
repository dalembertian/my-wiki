## How to read it

Treat it as a snapshot of the consensus canon at some point before the ChatGPT era, not as a current account:

- **It's stale.** GPT-3 is OpenAI's headline achievement, LeCun is placed "at Facebook" (Meta since 2021), and Musk appears as an OpenAI backer, although he left its board in 2018. Nothing after 2022 appears: no ChatGPT, no Anthropic, no NVIDIA, no mention of Hinton and Hassabis's 2024 Nobel prizes.
- **It flattens credit.** Backpropagation is credited to Hinton alone. The 1986 paper that popularised it was by Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams, and the idea is older still.
- **It mixes kinds.** Seven entries are people or labs doing research. IBM Watson is a product brand, and Musk is on the list for funding and advocacy rather than technical work.
- **It pads itself out.** The closing paragraph is printed twice, followed by an invitation to email in if "you or your company should be included". That reads more like a directory than editorial judgement.

What it gets right is the core: Hinton, Bengio, LeCun, Li and Hassabis are the names every account of the deep-learning era shares, and that consensus is the useful thing a list like this records.
